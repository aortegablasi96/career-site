## Business Case:

### Problem

Coin collectors often manage their collections across spreadsheets, cloud storage and physical documents, making it difficult to maintain a complete view of their inventory, associated purchase documentation and collection value.

### Product decision

Build a cloud-based SaaS application that centralizes the management of a coin collection in a structured workspace.

Collectors can store and classify coins, associate images and purchase documentation with individual items, and explore collection analytics such as acquisition costs.

An AI assistant was added to enable natural-language exploration of the collector's own data.

### Key Product Decisions

- **Cloud-first architecture**: Enable persistent access to collection data and media across devices.
- **Structured collection model**: Represent coins and their attributes as structured data rather than unstructured notes or spreadsheets.
- **Integrated documentation**: Keep coin information, images and purchase documentation connected within the same workflow.
- **AI grounded in collection data**: Use the collector's own data as context for natural-language exploration.

### Outcome

- Delivered and deployed a working coin collection management SaaS MVP.
- Centralized coin metadata, images, purchase information and associated documentation in an intuitive UX.
- Enabled natural-language exploration of collection data through an AI assistant.
- Established a data and AI architecture that enables future capabilities such as auction discovery and market-to-collection value analysis.
